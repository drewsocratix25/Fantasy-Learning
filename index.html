Why we made this

Little Wonders started at our kitchen table. My daughter loved music games, but everything we found was stuffed with ads, locked to one app store, or only pretending to be educational. So we made our own, together. She picked the princesses and the songs; I did the typing.

Now we're sharing it. Every game here is simple, genuinely educational, and built for small hands and short attention spans. There are no ads, ever. We don't sell your data or collect anything about your child. And because it runs in the browser, it plays on whatever you have handy: an iPad, a phone, a laptop, or Grandma's computer. When one device dies, just open it on the next one.

If you'd like to support the project, it's $2 a month or $10 a year. That helps us pay for the servers and keep making new games. Think of us as a very small dad-and-daughter business.
