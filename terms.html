Terms

The plain-English version, last updated 5 September 2026.

The games are free

Every game on Little Wonders is free to play, with no ads and nothing locked behind payment. You may use them at home, in a classroom, or anywhere else, for as long as we keep the site up.

Supporting the project

Support is a voluntary subscription of $2 a month or $10 a year (in US dollars, plus any tax your region requires), billed by Stripe. It renews automatically until you cancel, which you can do at any time from the home page. Cancelling stops the next charge; we do not refund the current period, but if something went wrong just email us and we will sort it out. Supporting does not buy features, only a thank-you badge and our gratitude.

Family codes

A family code is for one household. Anyone who has the code can see and change that family's game progress, so please do not post it publicly. We may delete families that have not been used for two years.

For grown-ups

Family codes and payments must be set up by an adult. The games are designed to be used by children with a grown-up nearby; they are a learning aid, not a substitute for teaching.

Content and ownership

The games, artwork and code are ours (or open source, as credited in the source repository). You may not resell the games or strip out our name, but you are welcome to link to them, screenshot them, and show them to anyone.

No guarantees

We are a very small dad-and-daughter business. We try hard to keep the site fast, safe and online, but we provide it "as is", without warranties, and our liability to you is limited to what you have paid us in the last twelve months.
